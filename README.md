# AI-Trainer

AI Image Trainer

Build and train your own AI image detector right in your browser. Fetch photos from Wikimedia Commons or upload your own, annotate objects, train, and test with a photo or live camera.

Everything stays in your browser, and nothing is uploaded online.

Built with TensorFlow.js.
